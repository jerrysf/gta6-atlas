Between trailers, leaks, and a decade of rumor, it can be hard to know what's actually real about Grand Theft Auto VI. This guide lists only information Rockstar Games has officially confirmed — through the GTA VI website, trailers, and official screenshots.

## The setting: Leonida

GTA VI takes place in the fictional state of **Leonida**, Rockstar's version of Florida. Officially confirmed areas include:

- **Vice City** — the neon-soaked metropolis returning in modern form.
- **Leonida Keys** — a tropical island chain where Jason Duval works.
- **Grassrivers** — vast wetland wilderness.
- **Port Gellhorn** — a faded coastal strip on the west side.
- **Ambrosia** — an industrial town built around the Allied Crystal sugar refinery.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park** — rugged northern backcountry.

See our [Leonida locations guide](/guides/leonida-locations-guide) for detail on each region.

## The protagonists

For the first time in the series' modern era, GTA VI features two playable leads:

- **Lucia Caminos** — a fighter fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary.
- **Jason Duval** — her partner, working odd jobs for Keys smugglers.

Their story is built around a criminal partnership — a modern Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic, per official materials. Meet the full cast in our [character guide](/guides/gta-6-characters-explained) and [character database](/characters).

## Release and platforms

GTA VI launches **November 19, 2026** on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. Read the full [release date guide](/guides/gta-6-release-date) for the delay history and platform details.

## Official materials so far

- **Trailer 1** (December 2023) — the reveal: Lucia, Vice City, Leonida.
- **Trailer 2** (May 2025) — Jason's story, new characters, deeper look at the world.
- **Extended Look gameplay reveal** — the first real gameplay, including weapon pickup prompts that officially named guns like the Duke 556 and Moreland 850.
- **Launch Trailer** — the final pre-release trailer, showing combat, pursuits and set pieces.
- **Official website** — character bios, location profiles, and screenshots at rockstargames.com/VI.

## Confirmed vehicles and weapons

Official footage has revealed dozens of vehicles — returning nameplates like the Banshee, Coquette, Caracara and Buzzard appear across trailers (names are community identifications until verified in-game). Several weapons have been officially **named on-screen**, including the Klose K17, Girardi ES9, Duke 556 Assault Rifle, Moreland 850 shotgun and the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers bundled with the Ultimate Edition.
