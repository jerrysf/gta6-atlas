## The honest answer

No official GTA 6 achievement or trophy list has been published. Rockstar lists typically go live **within days of launch** — sometimes appearing on PSN/Xbox a day or two early. Anything claiming a full leaked list right now is unverified.

## What history suggests — community expectation, not confirmation

Rockstar lists almost always include:

- **Story-completion achievements** — finish the campaign
- **A 100% completion achievement** ("Career Criminal"-style)
- **Collectible & side-content** achievements — stunt jumps, strangers, hidden packages-style sets
- **Online achievements** — if a successor to GTA Online ships
- **Miscellaneous feats** — heist variants, minigame wins, world interactions

GTA V shipped with 50 achievements; RDR2 with 51. Expect GTA 6 in the same ballpark.
