## The rating: M for Mature (17+)

Grand Theft Auto VI carries an **ESRB "M" rating**, confirmed via the content descriptors published alongside Rockstar's official videos.

## Confirmed content descriptors

Rockstar's official materials list:

- **Blood and Gore**
- **Intense Violence**
- **Mature Humor**
- **Nudity**
- **Strong Language**
- **Strong Sexual Content**
- **Use of Drugs and Alcohol**

## What that means in practice

This is the standard adult content profile for the series — consistent with GTA V and RDR2's ratings. For parents: the M rating means content intended for ages 17+, covering the full range of mature themes the franchise is known for.

## Regional ratings

- **ESRB (North America)**: M — Mature 17+
- **PEGI (Europe)**: expected 18 — regional boards publish ratings closer to or at launch
