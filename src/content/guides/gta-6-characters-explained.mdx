Rockstar's official GTA VI website and second trailer introduced the cast of Leonida. Here is every character confirmed so far — and only what we actually know about them.

## Lucia Caminos

One of the two playable protagonists. Lucia grew up fighting, taught by her father, and a run of bad luck landed her in **Leonida Penitentiary** — where her story begins. Out of prison and determined to change her odds, she partners with Jason and gets pulled into a conspiracy spanning the whole state.

## Jason Duval

The second playable protagonist. Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, did a stint in the Army, and ended up in the **Leonida Keys** running odd jobs for local smugglers. Meeting Lucia turns his small-time operation into something much bigger.

## Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and an associate of Brian Heder's operation. Cal is the group's stay-at-home conspiracy watcher — happiest tracking ships, planes and rumors from behind a screen. Official bios suggest he gets pulled deeper into Jason and Lucia's troubles than he ever wanted.

## Brian Heder

A Leonida Keys fixture who runs a boat yard and marina as a front for old-school drug running. Jason works jobs for him. Brian has spent years keeping the law guessing.

## Dre'Quan Priest

A former street dealer who went legit — or at least legit-adjacent — founding the **Only Raw Records** label in Vice City. Ambitious and calculating, Dre'Quan is always hunting the next big move.

## Boobie Ike

A Vice City legend who converted street credibility into a business empire: real estate, a strip club, a recording studio. He partners with Dre'Quan on Only Raw Records.

## Raul Bautista

A professional bank robber always scouting for talent — and always looking for a bigger score. His recruiting puts him on a collision course with the protagonists.

## Real Dimez

The rap duo of childhood friends **Bae-Luxe** and **Roxy**, signed to Only Raw Records and chasing their next hit in Vice City's music scene.
