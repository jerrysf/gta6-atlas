## What the Criminal Profile is

Reporting around GTA 6 describes a **Criminal Profile** system — a layer tracking how your criminal behaviour reads to the world of Leonida, on a spectrum from **Professional** to **Psycho**:

- **Professional** — clean, calculated crime; jobs done quietly
- **Psycho** — chaotic, high-body-count mayhem the city remembers

Think of it as the game's read on *how* you commit crime, not just *whether* you're wanted — a reputation layer sitting alongside the classic wanted stars.

## How it may interact with the wanted system

The six-star wanted level is a long-standing series staple and community reporting suggests it returns. The reported model:

- **Wanted stars** = immediate police response in the moment
- **Criminal Profile** = longer-term notoriety shaping how NPCs, fixers and the world treat you

Exact mechanics — how the profile rises, falls, and what it unlocks — haven't been officially documented.

## Why it matters for the story

Jason and Lucia's arc is framed around a criminal conspiracy across Leonida — a profile system fits a Bonnie-and-Clyde story where *reputation* is a resource: being known as pros opens doors; being known as unhinged closes them.

## Unconfirmed until launch

Specifics — visible profile meters, per-playstyle perks, how resetting works — are all details we'll document from gameplay after November 19, 2026. This page upgrades to verified status then.
