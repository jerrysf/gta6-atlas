## Which GTA 6 edition should you buy?

Grand Theft Auto VI is available for **pre-order now** ahead of the **November 19, 2026** launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here is what Rockstar has officially confirmed.

## Confirmed editions

### Standard Edition

The base game — full access to Jason and Lucia's story across Leonida, plus the standard GTA Online access that ships with the title.

### Ultimate Edition

Rockstar has announced an **Ultimate Edition** with in-game bonus content. Confirmed items include:

- **A matched pair of engraved revolvers** — a "his and hers" weapon set for the dual protagonists, seen in official weapon imagery.
- Additional in-game bonuses as listed on Rockstar's official store page.

We don't reproduce pricing here because it varies by region and storefront — check the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store or Rockstar's own site for current prices.

## Pre-order bonuses

Rockstar typically bundles a pre-order bonus (in-game cash or items). Details live on the official store page and platform storefronts — we'll update this page as bonuses are verified.
