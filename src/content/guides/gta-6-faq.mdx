This page collects the most common GTA 6 questions with answers sourced from official Rockstar materials only. For anything Rockstar hasn't confirmed, we say so plainly.

## Release and platforms

**When is GTA 6 coming out?** November 19, 2026 — the date Rockstar confirmed after moving it from fall 2025 and then May 26, 2026.

**Which platforms?** PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch. No PC date has been announced.

**How much will it cost?** Rockstar hasn't announced pricing or editions.

## Story and world

**Who do you play as?** Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — the series' first dual protagonists, a criminal couple in a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style story.

**Where is it set?** The state of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida. Confirmed regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Details in our [locations guide](/guides/leonida-locations-guide).

## Online and gameplay

**Will there be GTA Online for GTA VI?** Rockstar hasn't detailed the online component. GTA Online (for GTA V) continues as its own game.

**What about gameplay features?** Almost nothing mechanical has been officially detailed — no confirmed mission list, vehicle roster or weapon stats. We'll document everything once verified in-game, and our [completion tracker](/completion) and [walkthroughs](/walkthrough) go live at launch.
