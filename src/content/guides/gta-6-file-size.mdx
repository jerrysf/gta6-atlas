## Has Rockstar confirmed the GTA 6 file size?

**No.** As of now, Rockstar Games has not published an official download size for Grand Theft Auto VI. Any specific gigabyte number you see on social media or forums is **speculation, not a confirmed figure**.

The real number will appear on the PlayStation Store and Xbox store product pages — typically when pre-orders go fully live and again at pre-load.

## What the community estimates

Players estimate GTA 6's size from Rockstar's recent releases:

- **GTA V (PS5/Xbox Series X|S)** — roughly **110 GB**
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** — roughly **150 GB**
- **GTA V + GTA Online at peak** — over **180 GB** combined

Given GTA 6's scope — a full state of Leonida, denser cities and a new engine generation — community estimates commonly land in the **150–250 GB** range. This is an educated guess, not a leak or confirmation.

## How much free space to prepare

A practical approach ahead of launch:

- **Free up at least 250 GB** if you can — installs often need extra headroom for the download plus unpacking
- **Check expandable storage now** — PS5 accepts compatible NVMe drives; Xbox Series X|S uses official expansion cards or external USB drives for storage (note: Xbox requires Series-optimised games on internal/expansion storage to *play*)
- **Budget for the day-one patch** — launch updates add several GB on top (see our [day-one patch guide](/guides/gta-6-day-one-patch))

## When we'll know the real number

Console storefronts display the exact install size once pre-load begins — expected in the days before the **November 19, 2026** release. We'll update this page with the confirmed figure and break down the base game versus patch sizes the moment it's public.
