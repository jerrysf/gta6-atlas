## Confirmed and strongly-implied groups

Official GTA VI material — trailers, the Extended Look and Rockstar's site — has already sketched Leonida's underworld:

- **The Real Dimez** — Bae-Luxe and Roxy's rap duo climbing out of street life into the music scene; Jason and Lucia's circle overlaps theirs
- **Thrillbilly Mud Club** — Ambrosia's mudding and off-road crew, visible in trailer footage with monster trucks and swamp culture
- **Biker crews** — motorcycle clubs visible around Port Gellhorn and the highways
- **Raul Bautista's network** — the bank-robbing professional at the centre of the "criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida"
- **Brian Heder's operation** — the Keys smuggler Jason runs errands for; a local criminal enterprise rather than a gang

## Community-reported groups

Unconfirmed but widely speculated from footage analysis:

- **Haitian or Haitian-inspired crews** — Vice City precedent makes this a common community expectation, but nothing is confirmed
- **Cuban and Latin street crews** — plausible for Vice City's neighbourhoods, unverified
- **Corrupt officials** — the story synopsis implies institutional rot; specifics unknown

## What we don't know

Faction gameplay — joinable crews, territory systems, reputation — is completely unconfirmed. GTA V kept gangs mostly as world texture; whether GTA 6 deepens that is an open question until release.
