## The last trailer before launch

On **October 15, 2026** Rockstar dropped a ~90-second launch trailer — a mood piece rather than a deep gameplay dive, captured entirely on PS5.

## What it revealed

- **"Flower in a Hurricane"** — the debut track from original score composers **Justice**, featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It's also on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, the official soundtrack available for pre-save/pre-order.
- **A tightened story synopsis** — "an easy score goes wrong" and "a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida."
- **Vice City vibes** — a quick montage of beachfront, nightlife and the duo on the run, no major new locations revealed.

## What it didn't show

No new map reveals, no online mode, no mission structure. This was a tone-setting trailer — the substance shipped earlier:

- **[Trailer 2 breakdown](/guides/gta-6-trailer-2-breakdown)** — the big content trailer (May 2025)
- **An Extended Look** — the 26-minute gameplay feature (Aug 2026)

## The countdown

Roughly one month separated the launch trailer from release — GTA 6 arrives **November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Watch the clock on our [homepage](/).
