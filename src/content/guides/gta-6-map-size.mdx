## What's officially confirmed

Rockstar has profiled six named regions of the state of **Leonida** on the official GTA 6 site:

- **Vice City** — the neon metropolis, Leonida's Miami
- **Leonida Keys** — tropical archipelago, Jason's home turf
- **Grassrivers** — wetlands and swamps
- **Port Gellhorn** — faded coastal town
- **Ambrosia** — agricultural heartland
- **Mount Kalaga National Park** — highlands and wilderness

## How big is it, really?

Rockstar has published **no official size figure**. Reports citing people familiar with the game describe Leonida as **roughly twice the size of GTA 5's Los Santos & Blaine County**, with new systems like hurricanes and tropical storms.

Treat all square-mile numbers floating around as **community estimates** — nobody outside Rockstar has measured the real map.
