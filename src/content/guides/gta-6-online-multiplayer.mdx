## What's officially known — very little

Rockstar has confirmed Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player story about Jason and Lucia launching November 19, 2026. **No details about an online mode have been announced.**

## The community expectation — unconfirmed

Given that GTA Online became one of the most profitable live services ever, a successor mode alongside GTA 6 is the broad industry expectation:

- A **GTA Online successor** set in Leonida is widely anticipated but **not announced**.
- GTA V's online continued alongside the story at launch; a similar structure is expected — unconfirmed.
- No details exist on cross-progression, platforms or launch timing for online.

## What the Extended Look showed

The 26-minute Extended Look focused on single-player systems — character swapping between Lucia and Jason, activities and wanted-level mechanics. No multiplayer footage has been shown officially.

## Bottom line

Treat any site claiming confirmed GTA 6 Online details as speculation until Rockstar announces it. We'll update the moment official news drops.
