## The official answer

Rockstar Games has confirmed **Grand Theft Auto VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only**, launching **November 19, 2026**. No PC version has been officially announced or dated.

## What history suggests — not official

Rockstar's past release pattern gives the community a rough expectation:

| Game | Console launch | PC launch | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA V | Sep 2013 | Apr 2015 | ~19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | Oct 2018 | Nov 2019 | ~13 months |

Applying the same pattern to GTA 6 points to a **late 2027 – mid 2028** PC window. That is a **community projection, not an official date** — Rockstar could move faster or slower.

## Why PC players should still watch the console launch

- Everything verified on console (map, missions, mechanics) will carry to PC whenever it ships.
- Modding and performance questions can't be answered until a PC build exists — ignore sites claiming confirmed specs.

## Bottom line

If you want GTA 6 on day one, it's console or nothing. For PC, expect a wait measured in years rather than months — and we'll update this page the moment Rockstar says otherwise.
