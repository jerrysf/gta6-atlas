## The honest answer: no official specs exist

Rockstar Games has **not announced a PC version** of Grand Theft Auto VI, so there are **no official system requirements**. Any website listing "minimum" and "recommended" specs today is inventing numbers.

Confirmed platforms for the November 19, 2026 launch: **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. See our [PC release guide](/guides/gta-6-pc-release) for what history suggests about timing.

## What the community expects

Predictions are educated guesses built on:

- **The RAGE engine's history** — GTA V and RDR2 scaled well but demanded strong CPUs at high settings
- **Console hardware as the baseline** — GTA 6 targets PS5/Series X class hardware, so a comparable PC build is the reasonable floor
- **Trailer fidelity** — the density and lighting in official footage suggest a demanding title at max settings

Common community predictions float around a modern 8-core CPU, 16 GB RAM and a current-generation mid-range GPU for comfortable play — but these are **estimates, not requirements**.

## Don't buy hardware on rumours

If you're planning a PC build for GTA 6:

- **Wait for the official spec sheet** — it arrives with the PC announcement
- **16–32 GB RAM and a current-gen GPU** is a safe general-purpose target regardless
- **Plan storage for a large install** — see our [file size guide](/guides/gta-6-file-size) for what the community estimates

## What we know for consoles

No specs needed — the game is built for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S specifically. Rockstar's Extended Look footage was captured on PS5, and performance targets (resolution/framerate modes) will be confirmed closer to launch.
