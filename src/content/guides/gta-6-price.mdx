## Official price: not announced

Rockstar Games has not published GTA 6 pricing. The PlayStation Store and Xbox store will carry the confirmed figures — and retailers list placeholder prices that are **not official**.

## What pricing looks like in 2026

Flagship game pricing has settled into clear tiers:

- **Standard editions** — $69.99–$79.99 is the current flagship norm
- **Deluxe/Ultimate editions** — $99.99–$119.99
- **Collector's editions** — $150+ when physical extras ship

GTA 6 is the industry's biggest release — expect top-of-range pricing, but the $100-base-price rumour is **speculation**, not confirmation.
