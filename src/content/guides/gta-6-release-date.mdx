## The official release date

Grand Theft Auto VI releases **November 19, 2026**, according to Rockstar Games' official announcements. The game launches on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**.

## Delay history

Rockstar has moved the date more than once:

- **Fall 2025** — the original window given when the first trailer dropped in December 2023.
- **May 26, 2026** — announced alongside the second trailer in May 2025.
- **November 19, 2026** — the current date, announced in a later update as Rockstar asked for more time to polish the game.

## Platforms

Confirmed platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. Historically, Rockstar's major releases have arrived on PC after the console launch, but nothing is official until Rockstar says so — treat any claimed PC date as speculation.
