## The official synopsis

> Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.

That's Rockstar's own framing — released alongside the Launch Trailer.

## The protagonists

- **Lucia Caminos** — released from Leonida Penitentiary at the start of the story. Trailer footage shows her in prison orange, then free and rebuilding.
- **Jason Duval** — runs errands for smuggler **Brian Heder** out of the Leonida Keys. A small-time operator pulled into something bigger.

Their dynamic — explicitly compared to Bonnie & Clyde — drives the dual-protagonist system. [Full character list →](/characters)

## The confirmed plot beats

1. Lucia's release from [Leonida Penitentiary](/locations/leonida-penitentiary)
2. An "easy score" robbery that goes sideways — the Trailer 2 vault job
3. A conspiracy "stretching across the state" — from [Vice City](/locations/vice-city) to [Port Gellhorn](/locations/port-gellhorn)
4. Support cast: fixer **Raul Bautista**, musician **Dre'Quan Priest**, mogul **Boobie Ike**, the **Real Dimez** duo, conspiracy theorist **Cal Hampton**, smuggler **Brian Heder**

## What we don't know

Mission-by-mission structure, choices, and endings are all unannounced — any site claiming a leaked full plot is speculating. We'll publish verified walkthroughs at launch.
