Trailer 2 (May 2025) was Rockstar's deepest look at GTA VI — two minutes packed with vehicles, weapons, locations and world details. This guide collects what the community has documented, marked honestly: sightings are real, but in-game names remain unconfirmed until launch.

## Opening: the Leonida Keys

The trailer opens in the Keys — Jason fixing a leak at his waterside place before Brian Heder pulls up in a **Vapid Caracara** pickup. Marinas, jet skis, dinghies and a **Speedophile Seashark** establish the laid-back smuggling hub Jason calls home.

## Vice City streets

Jason's errands cut through Vice City traffic: a **Maibatsu Penumbra** parked curbside, a blue **Invetero Coquette** on the highway, **Bravado Banshee** and **Pfister Growler** sports cars downtown, and an **Emperor Vectre** in the mix. A FlyUS-liveried **Jet** roars low over the gantries while the **VCMM Metro Mule** glides overhead.

## The law arrives

The Vice City Police Department gets real screen time: **Emperor 'La Perle'-style cruisers**, a **Vapid Montagne** police SUV, the **Bravado Buffalo STX** pursuit sedan, a **Police Maverick** overhead — and the **Brute Police Riot** armored truck taking a sedan through its side during the big chase.

## Businesses and brands

Trailer 2 is dense with world-building signage: **Phil's Ammu-Nation** (with Phil's ad voiceover), **Hank's Waffles**, the **Starlet Motel** in Port Gellhorn, **Patriot Beer**, and **Leonida Lottery** ads on ATMs. Each is catalogued in our [locations database](/locations).

## Weapons on show

Lucia shoulders a **cylinder-fed grenade launcher** in the pursuit; both leads fire an **MP5-style SMG** during the bank robbery; a **belt-fed machine gun** sits on the Ammu-Nation counter. Later materials added named weapons like the **Klose K17**, **Girardi ES9**, **Duke 556** and **Moreland 850** — full list in the [weapons database](/weapons).

## Off the beaten path

Grassrivers sequences show **airboats** skimming the wetlands and the **Nagasaki Buzzard Attack Chopper** overhead; Keys water shows **seaplanes** and sailing yachts; and backcountry shots tease the **Sanchez** dirt bike around Mount Kalaga.

## What we still don't know

No mission names, no HUD-level mechanics detail beyond what the Extended Look showed, no map. Everything here comes from footage analysis — treat names as placeholders until verified. When the game lands November 19, every entry upgrades from community-reported to gameplay-verified.
