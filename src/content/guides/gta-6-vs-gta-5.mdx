GTA 5 launched in 2013 and became the most profitable entertainment product
ever made. GTA 6 is its first mainline successor in 13 years — here's how the
two compare on everything that's actually confirmed.

## Setting: Leonida vs San Andreas

GTA 5 was set in **San Andreas** — a fictional California anchored by Los
Santos, with a countryside of desert, mountains and forest.

GTA 6 moves to the state of **Leonida** — a fictional Florida. Its anchor city
is a modern-day **Vice City**, but the state also includes the Leonida Keys,
the Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Trailer
footage shows dense urban cores, swamp waterways, tropical coastline and rural
interior — a wider biome range than GTA 5's single metro + countryside.

Rockstar has not released official map dimensions. Community comparisons of
landmark distances suggest Leonida is **significantly larger**, but treat any
specific size figure as an estimate.

## Protagonists: two vs three

| | GTA 6 | GTA 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Playable leads | **Lucia Caminos & Jason Duval** | Michael, Franklin, Trevor |
| Premise | A criminal couple on the run | Three career criminals' intersecting lives |
| Switching | Confirmed to return | Instant three-way switching |

The two-lead structure is a first for the series — a Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic
rather than GTA 5's ensemble. Lucia is also the first female protagonist in a
mainline 3D GTA.

## Graphics and technology

Thirteen years of hardware generations separate the two:

- **GTA 5** shipped on PS3/Xbox 360 and was later ported twice.
- **GTA 6** is built for **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only** — no last-gen
  version — with a new RAGE engine generation.

Trailer footage shows dense crowds, simulated hair and fabric, volumetric
weather and detailed interiors — with Rockstar's stated aim of "the biggest,
most immersive evolution" of the series. A PC version is not dated.

## The world feels alive differently

Confirmed and documented differences in world detail:

- **Social media layer** — in-game platforms (parodies of TikTok/Instagram)
  appear throughout the trailers, a mechanic GTA 5 predated.
- **Wildlife** — alligators, flamingos, dolphins and more confirmed in Leonida,
  a deeper ecosystem than GTA 5's sharks and cougars.
- **Criminal profile system** — a visible notoriety level (Professional →
  Psycho) that appears to drive how the world reacts to you.
- **Weather** — storms and hurricane conditions in a Florida-inspired setting.

## Online

GTA Online (2013) grew into a separate live-service product. GTA 6 will have
its own online component — details are unannounced. Rockstar has confirmed
GTA Online continues to operate, and there is **no announced carry-over** of
characters, progress or purchases between the two.

## What stays the same

The series' core grammar is intact: an open state to roam, driving-focused
traversal, crime-driven missions, a satirical American setting, and a deep
bench of side activities. GTA 6's differences are evolutionary — bigger
world, denser systems, two leads — not a reinvention.

## The short version

GTA 6 is GTA 5's formula at a generational leap: a larger, more detailed
state; two protagonists instead of three; current-gen-only hardware; and a
living world layered with social media, wildlife and notoriety systems that
didn't exist in 2013.
