## Every GTA 6 weapon documented so far

20 weapons are visible across the trailers, screenshots and Extended Look. **8 carry officially shown names**; the rest are community identifications. See the full [weapon database](/weapons).

## Officially named

- **Klose K17** — Lucia's Glock-style sidearm
- **Girardi ES9** — Jason's engraved Beretta-style pistol
- **Duke 556 Assault Rifle** — the workhorse carbine
- **Moreland 850** — pump shotgun from the raid footage
- **Hawk & Little Morgan**, **Morgan Revolver**, **Mustang .357**, **Nipper .38** — four named handguns
- The **Ultimate Edition revolver pair** — his-and-hers engraved wheelguns

## Community-identified

- **MP5-style SMG** — bank robbery footage
- **Milkor-style grenade launcher** — the pursuit truck flip
- **AK-pattern rifle** (incl. a gold-plated variant), **bolt-action hunting rifle**, **Duke marksman rifle**
- **Double-barreled shotgun**, **belt-fed machine gun**, **machine pistol**, **compensated pistol**
- **Molotov**, baseball bat & melee weapons

## Where to buy them

[Phil's Ammu-Nation](/locations/phils-ammu-nation) is the confirmed gun shop — "more guns than the law allows," per his own ad.
