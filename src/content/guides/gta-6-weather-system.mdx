## What official footage shows

Grand Theft Auto VI's marketing footage has already shown Leonida under dramatically different skies:

- **Storm sequences** — heavy downpours over Vice City in trailer shots
- **Wind** — palm trees bending and debris moving in storm conditions
- **Rough seas** — choppy water around the Leonida Keys versus glassy calm in other shots
- **Atmospheric lighting** — golden-hour, overcast and neon-lit night conditions all confirmed visually

The "Flower in a Hurricane" Launch Trailer title is itself a nod to Florida's storm season.

## Hurricanes in Leonida?

Florida means hurricanes — and Leonida is Florida. Community analysis of footage has focused on whether GTA 6 simulates hurricane conditions:

- Storm intensity in some shots goes well beyond GTA V's rain
- Whether storms arrive as **scripted story events**, a **dynamic weather cycle**, or both hasn't been detailed by Rockstar

Treat any specific claim — named storms, evacuation events, flooding mechanics — as **unconfirmed speculation** until gameplay details or release verify it.

## What it could mean for gameplay

Community expectations, unverified:

- Flooded low-lying streets in Port Gellhorn and the Keys during storms
- Rougher seas making boat handling harder
- Weather driving wildlife and NPC behaviour indoors

On November 19, 2026 we'll document the actual weather cycle, whether storms are dynamic or scripted, and any gameplay effects — and update this page to verified status.
