GTA VI launches November 19, 2026. This checklist covers the practical stuff — what's confirmed, what to expect, and how to be ready on day one.

## Hardware and platform

- **Confirmed platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no last-gen (PS4/Xbox One) version.
- **Storage:** GTA titles are large. Ensure you have free disk space before launch; an SSD is standard on both target platforms.
- **PC players:** no PC version is dated. Watch Rockstar's announcements rather than rumors.

## A note on GTA Online

GTA Online for GTA V continues to operate independently. Rockstar has not detailed GTA VI's online component — expect official news through Rockstar Newswire, and avoid trusting unofficial "GTA 6 Online beta" claims, which are a common scam vector.

## Day-one tips (franchise experience, not GTA 6 specifics)

These are general GTA habits that hold across the series — GTA VI's specifics will be verified at launch:

- Follow the early story missions before free-roaming; they unlock the world's systems.
- Save different slots at major branches in case you want to replay missions.
- Explore on foot occasionally — collectibles and secrets hide off the road.
