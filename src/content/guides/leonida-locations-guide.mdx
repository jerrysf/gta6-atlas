GTA VI's world is the state of **Leonida** — Rockstar's fictional Florida. The official GTA VI website profiles six areas. Here's what Rockstar has confirmed about each, with links to their database entries.

## Vice City

The centerpiece: a modern reimagining of Rockstar's Miami-inspired metropolis. Official materials show the neon beachfront, high-rise downtown, working-class neighborhoods and the surrounding sprawl. [View Vice City →](/locations/vice-city)

## Leonida Keys

A chain of tropical islands south of the mainland — marinas, boat yards, tiki bars and easy living with a smuggling undercurrent. This is Jason Duval's home turf and where Brian Heder runs his operation. [View the Leonida Keys →](/locations/leonida-keys)

## Grassrivers

Leonida's vast wetlands — sawgrass channels, gators and backcountry waterways. Think of it as the state's untamed interior. [View the Grassrivers →](/locations/grassrivers)

## Port Gellhorn

A faded coastal town on Leonida's west side: cheap motels, shuttered attractions and empty storefronts — the strip the tourists forgot. [View Port Gellhorn →](/locations/port-gellhorn)

## Ambrosia

An industrial heartland town dominated by the **Allied Crystal sugar refinery**. Official materials describe it as hard-labor country — old money, local power struggles and biker turf. [View Ambrosia →](/locations/ambrosia)

## Mount Kalaga National Park

The wilderness on Leonida's northern border — dense forest, river gorges and backcountry trails for hunting, fishing and off-roading. [View Mount Kalaga →](/locations/mount-kalaga)
